Decisions

Harvest

The FarmKuest approach

A conversation.
Not another form.

Turn everyday replies into useful farm records. Clarify missing information instead of inventing it.

01 · Register the plot and crop

Start with planting details and location, with field-officer assistance when needed.

02 · Send a useful check-in

Combine reviewed schedules and weather information. No reply stays unconfirmed.

03 · Keep the season's history

Record observations, inputs, harvests, sales, and payments as separate events.

The venture hypothesis

Useful to the farmer.
Valuable to the network.

Customer

Farmers use it. Partners may fund it.

Test subscriptions with cooperatives, grower groups, and off-takers that need better production records. No revenue or paying customers are claimed.

Differentiation

Recordkeeping through conversation.

Combine low-friction check-ins, crop context, and harvest-to-payment history. Validate the advantage through farmer interviews and competitor research.

Trust

Keep the unknowns visible.

Separate forecasts from measurements and self-reports from verified facts. Share farmer records only with permission.

Why an AI bootcamp?

Test the hard part.
Not just the dashboard.

Interpret short, local, imperfect messages reliably—and know when to ask another question.

Model strategy

Evaluate suitable Mistral models for structured extraction and grounded summaries. Measure accuracy, cost, and local-language performance.

Data strategy

Start with reviewed synthetic examples, then consented pilot messages. Test ambiguity handling and avoidance of unsupported agricultural advice.

Production path

Web portal → authenticated API → PostgreSQL → SMS, weather, and model services. Azure is a proposed hosting option, subject to eligible credits and operating costs.

Focused validation

One region. One crop.
One useful first season.

Proposed weeks 1–3

Proposed weeks 4–6

Test the end-to-end loop
